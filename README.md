# Fan Coloring

A small tool for filling a fan-shaped outline with image brushes. One HTML file, nothing to install.

**Use it online:** https://ningweiyun-hub.github.io/fan-coloring/

## How it works

1. Pick a brush (11 yarn brushes are built in), or choose **Import image** to add your own. You can mark which part of the image to use.
2. Choose a cell to fill it. With a mouse, hold and drag to fill several cells.
3. Turn on **Same letter** to fill every cell with that letter at once. **Fill by letter** puts brushes A–H on their matching cells.
4. **Export PNG** saves exactly what is on screen.

Works on phones and tablets. The interface is available in English, Dutch and Chinese.

Your brushes and progress stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.

# 扇形填色

把圖片當筆刷，填進扇形底圖的小工具。單一 HTML 檔，不用安裝，手機和平板也能用；介面有英文、荷蘭文、中文。

筆刷和填色進度只存在你自己的瀏覽器裡，不會上傳到任何地方。
